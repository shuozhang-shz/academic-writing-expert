# Changelog

## 1.1.0 — 2026-11-06

The skill can now draft and audit a journal cover letter from a manuscript, the journal brief and confirmed author declarations. The Chinese feature guide lists the individual writing tasks and their supporting modules.

- Added CARS introduction drafting, cross-section evidence checks, traceable references, funding and acknowledgments.
- Added submission-package checks, revision letters and point-by-point responses.
- Included the earlier local updates for sentence and paragraph decisions and for titles, abstracts and keywords.
- Mapped the 138-page, 49-page and 39-page course PDFs to the relevant skill rules. Added an explicit check for attribution when editing a modifier.
- Reviewed three independent writing outputs covering a submission letter, a manuscript with inconsistent scope, and acknowledgments with conflicting reference records.

## 1.0.0 — 2026-10-03

Initial public release, including the academic-writing PPT expansion.

- Evidence-aware manuscript drafting, revision, English polishing, and Chinese-to-English rewriting.
- Section guidance, scientific invariants, and an optional manuscript dossier.
- Detailed grammar and punctuation decisions covering the supplied presentation's usage rules.
- Source mapping for the two PDFs and all 46 slides, with contextual treatment of historical advice.
- Bilingual installation and usage documentation, MIT license, and attribution notice.

The original source documents and local validation artifacts are excluded from the distribution.
